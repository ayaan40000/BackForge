# Changelog

All notable changes to BackForge. Versions follow the roadmap in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## v4.5 — Live Studio (2026-11-23)

A new app. The v4.0 single file moved to `/classic` (still used for the check-ups); the root is now a rebuilt, design-led app sharing the same data.

- **New look and shell.** Five places: Today, Live, Moves, Progress, You. Phones get a glass tab bar, tablets a rail, desktops a sidebar with two or three columns. Liquid-glass surfaces sit over a slowly drifting light background, in dark and light themes, with smooth screen transitions. "Reduce motion" and "Solid surfaces" are under You. Built from `docs/DESIGN-PROTOCOL.md`, written by a design lead with connected-fitness and digital-physio experience.
- **Live is live.** Choose 5–60 minutes, your energy, and any adjustments; all three are pre-filled. BackForge builds the session second by second (warm-up, main work, cool-down, sets, left and right sides, rests) and fits it to the time you chose. Press Start and it runs by itself.
- **A voice coach.** It talks you through the session like a personal trainer: what the move is, how to set up, what to focus on, the countdown, a form reminder, "two more", praise in the rests, a pain check, and what's coming next. It never talks over the countdown. Captions show every line, so it also works muted.
- **Built for the floor.** The Pain button is always on screen: sharp or leg pain stops that move, and warning signs stop the session with urgent-care advice. "Easier" swaps to the regression for the rest of the move, and "+15 s" adds rest. Headphone buttons pause and skip. The screen stays on, an optional generated music bed plays underneath, and a session resumes where you left off if it's interrupted.
- **Today** shows readiness as a ring with its reasons, then the one session with a Start button. Next come posture cues and a health strip (steps, sleep stages, resting HR, HRV, Zone 2, weight), then this week. You check in with one tap on a 0–10 scale.
- **Progress** has four sections. Overview: pain trend, a 17-week consistency map, sessions per week, the progression gate, insights that state how much data they're based on, and what the coach has learned. Health: steps, sleep stages, resting HR and HRV against your normal range, Zone 2 per week, and recent workouts. Body: weight trend, energy balance, waist, protein. Posture: findings and assessment history.
- **Demo data.** You → Data → "Preview with demo data" (or `?demo=1`) shows the app with 120 days of example history, including Google Fit and Samsung Health data. It is stored separately and never mixed with your data.
- **Health sync v3.** The import now also accepts resting HR, HRV, sleep stages, VO₂ max, SpO₂, body fat and workouts. Older sync files still import.
- **For maintainers.** `AGENTS.md` explains how Muse or any AI can maintain the app. Coach lines, cues and coaching notes are data files. 19 new tests cover the safety rules for Live, duration fitting, the coach's timing and wording rules, the data files and the health import. A 42-check browser smoke test covers phone, tablet and desktop. Classic harnesses still pass (113 + 50), and the backend still passes (81).
- Safety rules carry over unchanged and are re-tested on every Live build. The flare protocol drops the side-bend, as it did in v4.0.

## v4.0 — The Clinic Edition (2026-11-22)

Ships the roadmap's v3.5 + v4.0 owner scope in one release: posture grading, photo assessment, and the app becoming a clinic-grade instrument. Built by a four-expert design pass (posture science, exercise DB/videos, UX architecture, imaging/security/sync — specs in `dev/v4-specs/`) and hardened by three adversarial review iterations (scores and the path-to-8/10 plan: `docs/ROADMAP.md`).

- **App shell.** Five bottom tabs — Home, Train, Posture, Moves, Progress — with inline SVG icons; Profile (name, PIN, body details, travel mode, reminder, data) moved behind a header avatar; segmented in-place panels on Posture (Today / Assess / Photo check) and Progress (Overview / Health / Weight); per-tab scroll restore; the 13 movement tests paginated one at a time. All v3 engines (assessment, plan generator, Live builder, player, gate) untouched in behaviour.
- **Daily posture cues (Posture → Today, and one card on Home).** All 16 of the posture spec's cues (8 anterior-pelvic-tilt, 8 forward-head/rounded-shoulder) verbatim, one tap to mark caught, shared state between Home and Posture; the daily set of 4 tilts toward whichever pattern graded worse at the last band check. The cooking/standing cue carries the owner's known ~2-hour standing trigger with a sit-early instruction.
- **Band-graded posture assessment (Posture → Assess).** Eight band tests (tilt control, banded dead bug, banded hip IR per side, Pallof hold, glute-med wall press, pull-apart endurance, banded wall slide, banded chin tuck) with multiple-choice inputs graded Clear / Mild / Moderate / Priority by the spec's rubrics. Pain gate: leg or sharp/electric pain stops the test at Priority and flags the physio; back-only ache caps the grade at Moderate. Grades emit findings into the same engine the movement assessment uses, and the plan consequences are wired, not decorative: tilt drill and dead-bug regression by grade, daily IR block with the deficit side named, glute-med supported-only work at Priority, Pallof half-kneeling + extra bird-dog set at Priority, band promotions blocked for pull-apart/face-pull (T6 Priority) and pull-through (T1 Priority), 90/90 lift-off progression gated at IR Priority. Answers save per step (resume after closing), a partial retest merges with the previous check — grades *and pain history* carry with their dates — and an unanswered test records nothing. Retest every 4 weeks (2 after a Priority; locked 7 days after a pain-gated test; never on a flare day — the wizard hard-stops, and the travel reset routes to relief).
- **Exercise database (Moves).** All 40 exercises carry what-it-helps, target muscles, the posture improvement it drives, an objective "you've got it" marker, hold/band targets, and contexts (home/desk/travel) with context filters and travel-mode sorting. Videos are popup click-to-play through the privacy-enhanced player: 5 YouTube videos verified at build time (Squat University Big 3, Bob & Brad, Live Lean TV, Chad G/Ortho OT, Christina Nicci) plus clearly labelled search links — no invented video IDs, per repo rule.
- **Photo posture check (Posture → Photo check).** Exactly four views (right side, left side, front, back) with stance/framing guidance, a last-time ghost for framing, EXIF-stripped normalisation, and on-device storage (IndexedDB; metadata only in state). "Prepare for Muse" downloads a Spec-shaped request (context, per-view dimensions, missing views, the screening prompt) and the photos themselves under the exact filenames the request declares; Muse's returned JSON imports with closed-enum validation and is recorded as an observation — it never changes the plan by itself.
- **Encrypted photo vault.** AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 (600k) key from a separate ≥12-character passphrase (never the PIN), fresh IV per file, file-id-bound AAD, 10-minute auto-lock. Export produces ciphertext-only `.enc.json` files the owner commits to `vault/` himself (the app holds no GitHub credentials). Restore works on a fresh device: the envelope carries its salt, iterations and view label; import derives per-envelope keys, reports failures by file id, and re-creates the vault from a successfully decrypted file. The spec's five-line threat model ships verbatim in the card.
- **Profile + PIN gate.** Local profile (display name, height, birth year) and an optional 4-digit PIN: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 (310k) salted hash only — no PIN value exists anywhere in the shipped code; the owner sets his own at first run (a one-time offer sheet, also reachable in Profile). Persisted escalating lockout (30 s → 5 min → 30 min steps to a 2 h cap), constant-time compare, app content hidden behind the gate, and the honest one-liner in-app: the PIN is a privacy screen, not encryption. PIN material is stripped from every export.
- **Sync with Muse v2 + body plan (Progress → Health / Weight).** "Sync with Muse" buttons create Spec-shaped sync requests (health or calories); imports accept v1 and v2 payloads, merge calories only when they are valid numbers (never faked, never zero-filled), validate every field (real calendar dates, per-field maxima), and match replies to requests. Health tiles + charts for steps, Zone 2, sleep and calories plot days-with-data only. The body plan combines the posture program with weight loss: Mifflin–St Jeor TDEE (step-derived activity, or solved from real intake + weight change when ≥14 days of both exist), a gentle deficit floored at BMR and 1,500 kcal, the 1.6 g/kg protein floor shown first, manual weight logging — and a pain guardrail: when the last 14 days run hot, the card prescribes maintenance and says why. Pause and delete now cover calories too, everywhere.
- **Safety invariants re-verified, plus two fixes:** QL side-bend removed from both flare protocols (side flexion can trigger this user's spasms — it stays as gentle unloaded mobility in cool-downs only), and the desk & travel reset is now genuinely all seated/standing. No press-ups or extension-loaded moves exist in the library; the extension-intolerant removals, skip = no data, and pain ≥ 7 = relief only all hold.
- **State schema 3.** Additive over schema 2 (profile, posture, vault, sync requests, nutrition, weight log, calorie plan, travel mode, UI state); partial or corrupt v4 subfields are type-repaired on every load. Full wipe now clears localStorage, the premigration copy, *and* the IndexedDB photo store.
- **Verified:** JS syntax on all 7 script blocks; `dev/harness-v4.mjs` 113/113 (schema 2→3 migration losslessness, grading tables + spec worked examples, wizard completeness/pain-gate/merge, cues, PIN hash/lockout/export-stripping, vault AES-GCM round-trips incl. a 300 KB buffer, sync v1+v2 import validation, poisoned-payload and corrupt-state probes, posture-result import, body-plan guardrails, travel reset composition, first-run PIN offer, flare routing); `dev/harness-v3.mjs` 50/50 (its three schema-version assertions updated to schema 3). Three adversarial iterations by an independent reviewer agent: 5.4 → 5.8 → 6.4 / 10, every critical and major finding fixed (see `docs/ROADMAP.md` for the score record and what remains on the path to 8/10). Not covered by any harness: the IndexedDB photo journey end-to-end on a real device, and the 390×844 viewport budget — both are itemised in the roadmap's path to 8/10.

## v3.0 — The Body, Connected (2026-11-17)

- **Health Connect sync via Muse (Progress → Health sync).** Muse reads Health Connect through its connector (Galaxy Watch + phone) and builds one JSON payload with `tools/health_sync.py`: per-day steps, Zone 2 minutes, sleep minutes/deep minutes, and scale weigh-ins for the last 28 days. The user imports the file (or pastes the JSON) in the app; days merge by date. Sync is read-only (check-ins, log and plan are never touched), pausable (paused data is invisible to readiness, the gate and trends), and fully deletable (one tap wipes synced data only). Honest limits are printed in the app: the connector exposes no raw heart-rate samples, so Zone 2 minutes count logged workout sessions whose average HR sits in the user's Zone 2 band (60–70% of estimated HRmax 220 − age; band and method are recorded in every payload) — a floor, not a total; weight is only as dense as the scale's own sync.
- **Recovery-aware planning.** The rolling 14-day progression gate gains two recovery criteria alongside pain ≤ 3 and 8+ sessions: synced step average and logged Zone 2 minutes. They can only *hold* a promotion on clear evidence — at least 7 synced days in the window plus a clearly failing signal (steps averaging under 4,000/day, or zero Zone 2 minutes) — never on missing data; every criterion prints its own line in the gate card. Readiness also takes synced drivers: last night's sleep under 6 h (−10) or at least 7.5 h (+5), and a 9,000+ step yesterday (−5, "a big day on the legs trims today a notch").
- **Weight & waist trends (Progress).** Synced weigh-ins and manual waist entries (Health Connect carries no waist data — the app says so and takes one number a week) charted against the pain trend. The read is weekly-bucket Pearson correlation with a hard honesty floor: under 4 paired weeks it prints "not enough overlapping data" and no number; above that it gives the strength tier and r value with a standing correlation-is-not-causation note.
- **Desk-day defense (Live).** A five-minute standing/posture block (chin tucks, wall angels, hip-flexor stretch, pull-aparts, 90/90 march — all existing library moves) offered as a Live session variant and logged as its own session title. Detection is stated plainly in the app: BackForge cannot read phone sedentary alerts, so a desk day is inferred from synced steps (a weekday under 3,000 steps, newest synced day within 2 days) or declared by the user. On a flare day the block is replaced by the relief protocol.
- **Data architecture step 1.** State carries `schema: 2` inside the unchanged `backforge_v1` envelope. The schema 1 → 2 migration is additive and lossless (health/waist/backup added; every v1/v2.5 field untouched), keeps a pristine premigration copy on the device (`backforge_v1_premigration`), and is idempotent. The weekly backup ritual (Progress → Your data is yours) exports a dated full-state JSON only after the user names where backups live — no destination named, no export — and stamps the last-export date with a due indicator. Still local-first; the app uploads nothing.
- **Backend:** `app/state.py` passthrough extended so the state import/export round-trip preserves `protein`, `bodyweightKg`, `deloadUntil` (v2.5 fields it previously dropped) and the v3.0 keys (`schema`, `health`, `waist`, `backup`, `migratedFrom`) when present; the empty-DB default export is byte-identical to before and the v:1 envelope check is unchanged.
- Verified: JS syntax on all 4 script blocks; 50-case jsdom harness over the production build (`dev/harness-v3.mjs`) — schema migration losslessness against a seeded v2.5 payload (every field compared, premigration copy byte-equal, idempotent reload), gate READY/hold matrix incl. recovery holds, thin-coverage and paused-sync cases, readiness synced drivers, fragment-night guard and pause invisibility, sync import/merge/refusal/pause/delete, trend honesty floor and 5-week correlation language, desk block composition + weekday rule + flare swap, backup destination enforcement, and the v2.5 regression set (skip = no data, readiness bands + flare override + stale confession, helps verdict, repair offer/break, deload proposal, protein math, builder flare/normal paths, weekly review) — 50/50. Backend suite 81 passing (78 + 3 new state-passthrough tests, no existing test modified). Sync generator: 21 unit tests passing (`tools/test_health_sync.py`), and the first real payload (28 days to 2026-11-17, 54 Zone 2 minutes, spot-checked against the connector: 2026-11-16 steps 14,304 exact, night ending 2026-11-17 deep sleep 149 min exact) ships in `tools/sample-payload/`.

## v2.5 — The Coach Gets a Memory (2026-11-03)

- **Readiness score (Today → Coach's read).** Sleep + today's pain + pain trend between check-ins + recent session load → a 0–100 morning readiness call (full / trimmed / micro / relief), shown with the full arithmetic — every point is explained, per the roadmap exit rule. Live Mode's default energy now derives from readiness: relief → flare protocol, micro → low, trimmed → okay. Pain ≥ 7 forces relief regardless of score.
- **Per-exercise response learning.** Each exercise is judged on next-morning pain after days it was logged (needs a check-in that day and the next): ≥2 readings and an average fall of 0.75+ → **Helps**; an average rise of 1.0+ → **Watch**; otherwise Neutral/Learning. The Live builder sorts Helps first and Watch last and prints the verdict sentence in "Why this workout?". All verdicts with their evidence live on Progress → "What the coach has learned".
- **Smart streak repair.** Missing exactly one day leaves the chain repairable: the Today card offers a repair session (smallest Live build). Logging it bridges the missed day at reduced credit — the missed day itself never counts, and two consecutive misses still break the chain. Repair sessions log as their own kind (`repair`).
- **Deload intelligence.** Weekly average pain compared across three week-windows: two consecutive rising weeks → deload proposed on Today; two falling weeks → retest invitation; a single rising week shows a plain watch note. An active deload week (7 days) trims the strength session to 3 exercises, drops one Live main exercise, caps sets at 2, and says so in every "Why" receipt.
- **Weekly review card (Progress).** Sessions this week vs last, average pain and its delta, gate status, one auto-picked win, one auto-picked adjustment, and a one-tap .txt export.
- **Protein floor tracker (Progress).** Bodyweight (lb) → floor of 1.6 g/kg/day; quick-add chips (+10/+25/+40 g) and custom grams; today's bar plus floor-hit days this week; low-protein weeks surface as the weekly review's adjustment. Photo-first logging is **not** in this version — it stays on the roadmap, and the in-app card says so.
- State: `backforge_v1` gains `protein`, `bodyweightKg`, `deloadUntil` — old saves merge over the defaults, nothing is lost. App footer version label bumped to v2.5 in the page itself.
- Verified: JS syntax on all 4 script blocks; 25-case functional harness over the extracted production functions (readiness bands incl. flare override and stale check-in, helps/watch/learning verdicts, repair offer + bridge + two-miss break, deload propose/retest/active/expired, protein target math, weekly review composition, builder integration incl. deload dose caps and flare relief-only, no extension-trap exercises served); v2.0.1 skip-fix regression re-run clean; backend suite unchanged at 78 passing (the engine port of the v2.5 logic is a follow-on, not in this release).

## v2.0.1 — Assessment skip fix (2026-11-02)

- **Fixed the phantom "Glutes are firing" finding in the app.** `scoreAssessment()` stored a skipped answer as the string `'skip'`, which is truthy in JS — so skipping every glute test (firing order, single-leg bridge, clamshell) satisfied the "glutes are firing" branch and reported a Clear finding from no data. The scorer now cleans `'skip'` to `null` for every answer before any check runs, exactly as the backend engine does. Stored answers are not changed; only scoring sees the cleaned copy.
- This restores the documented invariant in the live app: skipped tests are no data and can never generate a finding — including the partial case (one side skipped, the other clean no longer reports "firing"; both sides genuinely clean still does).
- Verified: JS syntax on all script blocks; functional scoring cases — all tests skipped → zero findings, glute tests only skipped → zero findings, clean glutes both sides → "Glutes are firing", two-hit right-side inhibition still detected, the full expected profile still scores its 10 findings; backend suite unchanged at 78 passing.

## v2.0 — Live Mode (2026-11-01)

- **Live workout partner (new Live tab).** Three taps — time on hand (5/10/20/35/50 min), body feel (great / okay / low energy / pain flare), and caveats (standing hurts, right/left side touchy, knees sore, no bands, quiet) — build a complete session: warm-up, main work, cool-down. One start button; no other decisions.
- **Self-updating defaults.** The default workout re-derives after every logged session and assessment from: assessment findings + severity, per-exercise logged history (new `S.exLog` — the guided player now records it too), today's pain/sleep check-in, and the A/B/C + Zone 2 weekly slot. A "Why this workout?" receipt explains every build in plain language.
- **Partner player.** Set × side steps with a coach cue on every step, hold timers, 45s auto-rests, progress bar, one-tap **Regress** (modify, don't stop), guilt-free **Skip** (skip = no data), resume after interruption (persisted in `S.livePlayer`), and a celebration finish: specific praise, a real-person quote, streak impact, and a one-tap pain score that feeds the progression gates.
- **Flare safety.** Flare energy (or a pain ≥ 7 check-in) runs the relief protocol only — Live Mode cannot build a strength session on a flare day.
- **Motivation system.** Daily rotating quotes from real, attributed people (James Clear, Mark Twain, Bruce Lee, Marcus Aurelius, Vince Lombardi, Muhammad Ali, Jim Rohn, Seneca, Confucius, Lao Tzu, Nietzsche, Henry Ford, Arnold Schwarzenegger, Jocko Willink, Karen Lamb, Jim Ryun, Will Durant on Aristotle); rotating coach notes on proven anti-procrastination mechanics (2-minute rule, don't-break-the-chain, implementation intentions, temptation bundling, future-self continuity, self-compassion research, identity voting).
- **Habit tracking.** Week dots (Mon–Sun chain view), milestone badges (first session, first Live workout, 3-day chain, week unbroken, 10/25/50 sessions, 14-day streak, gate opened) with a "next badge" target, and a done-today state that reframes extra work as bonus.
- Today view: a "already in the bank" encouragement line once today is logged.
- In-app roadmap cards rewritten to the version roadmap (v2.0 → v5.0). Full roadmap: `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
- Tested: JS syntax on all script blocks; jsdom functional suite in seeded (assessment + history) and fresh states — builder sections/budgets/caveats/flare, strength-day and rest-day paths, full player flow to logged session, regress path. All green.
