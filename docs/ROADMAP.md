# BackForge — Roadmap to v5.0

**Vision.** The back-rehab app a physiotherapist would hand to a patient and a strength coach would respect: assessment-led, safe by construction, private by architecture, and so easy to start that a busy, procrastinating person actually trains most days. It should feel like a coach is in the room.

**How to read this.** Only major releases are listed. Each one is a step change in what the product *is*, not a list of fixes. Bug fixes and small improvements ship continuously and go in `CHANGELOG.md`. Version history lives there too.

**What the review of the old plan found:**

- **The old roadmap ran out of road.** v4.5 "Circles" and v5.0 "Platform" were still a phone app with extras, and nothing in it addressed the two biggest gaps: the UX, and a Live mode that wasn't actually live.
- **Several "shipped" items were only partly delivered.** The v3.5 camera features (rep counting, form flags, camera-guided retest) were never built. The original v4.0 scope moved forward and was never done: clinician share, templates, multi-profile, audit trail and WCAG. Both are folded into the jumps below rather than kept as leftover lines.
- **The UX debt was structural, not cosmetic.** Ten tabs had become five but stayed text walls. Live needed a tap per step. There were two players with two states. Nothing fit on a phone. v4.5 fixes this by rebuilding the shell on a design protocol (`docs/DESIGN-PROTOCOL.md`) instead of patching it.
- **The path-to-8/10 debts from the v4.0 review are still open:** a vault end-to-end test, per-view photo checklists, video liveness checks, and real-device PIN and vault timing. They belong to the work that replaces those screens (v4.7), not to a separate cleanup release.

## v4.5 — LIVE STUDIO *(shipping 23 Nov 2026)*

**The jump: from a planner you read to a coach you press play on.**

- **A new app shell.** Five places: Today, Live, Moves, Progress, You. Phones get a bottom glass tab bar, tablets a rail, desktops a sidebar with 2–3 columns. Liquid-glass materials sit over a living ambient background, with oklch tokens in dark and light themes, View Transitions, `@starting-style` sheets and scroll-driven motion. The stack is Preact + htm, vendored with no build step.
- **Live in real time.** You pick a length from 5 to 60 minutes. The compiler turns the plan into a second-by-second timeline: warm-up, main work, cool-down, sets, sides and rests. It fits the chosen time by flexing rests and sets, never by changing a safety dose.
- **The voice coach.** It runs sessions the way trainers and physios do. It briefs each move (name, one setup cue, one focus cue), counts down, then gives a form cue at about a third and encouragement at about two thirds. It calls "last five" and "two more", praises specifically, coaches the breath in rests, checks pain after a move's first set, and previews the next move. It never talks over the countdown and drops lines that won't fit. The line library is in `data/voice-lines.json`.
- **Audio, phone and keyboard.** Cue tones are generated by Web Audio, with an optional music bed that dips while the coach speaks. Media Session gives headphone and lock-screen control, and Wake Lock keeps the screen on. Sessions resume after an interruption. The Pain button is always on screen. On desktop: Space, the arrow keys, E, R, V and P.
- **Demo mode.** 120 days of example history, including Google Fit and Samsung Health data (sleep stages, resting HR, HRV, VO₂ max, weight, Zone 2), so you can see the app full. It is kept in a separate storage key.
- **Built so Muse can maintain it.** Content is data files, the rules are in `AGENTS.md`, and 19 engine/live/data tests plus a 42-check browser smoke test run with no installs.
- **The classic v4.0 app** moved to `/classic`. It still hosts the check-ups, and both apps share the same data.

## v4.6 — ASSESS & CORRECT *(shipped 23 Nov 2026)*

**The jump: from a coach that runs a plan to one that measures what's weak, corrects it in phases, and checks the result.** The movement assessment, the posture check (8 band + 5 new bodyweight tests across four areas: pelvic tilt, forward head, hunched upper back, rounded shoulders), a phased corrective plan (Reset → Control → Strengthen → Integrate) run by the voice coach, in-session form ratings that regress or progress moves, and a photo check where Muse confirms the tests and tracks change. Details: `CHANGELOG.md`. This pulls the "check-ups rebuilt natively" item forward from v4.7. The encrypted vault and PIN setup still live in the classic app.

## v4.7 — THE COACH SEES AND HEARS *(target: Mar 2027)*

**The jump: the coach stops being a script and starts *responding*. It watches your form, hears you and reads your heart, all on the device.**

1. **On-device pose estimation, with nothing uploaded.** MediaPipe Tasks Vision runs in the browser through WebGPU/WASM, with the phone propped on the floor.
   - Rep counting for hinge, bridge, bird-dog and dead bug, so sets end when the reps are done and not when a timer says so.
   - Rehab-grade form flags: pelvis rotation on bird-dog, low-back arch at the top of a bridge, knee valgus in wall squats, chin poke on rows. The coach says them at the right moment in the rep ("hips level"), using the existing cue library.
   - Camera-guided check-ups, which replace the classic assessment, band check and photo check. Holds are timed, sides are measured symmetrically, and every value the camera proposes is confirmed by you.
2. **Two-way voice.** Speech recognition for "pause", "easier", "skip", "that's sharp" (opens the pain path) and "how long left". It also takes spoken morning check-ins ("pain three, slept badly"). Training becomes truly hands-free.
3. **Live heart rate.** Web Bluetooth connects to any standard heart-rate strap or watch broadcast. Rests become effort-adaptive (the next set starts when your heart rate has recovered, within limits), and Zone 2 minutes are measured during sessions, not estimated.
4. **The classic app retires.** The vault and PIN setup are rebuilt natively (the check-ups already moved in v4.6). The vault gets its end-to-end restore test, video links get a liveness checker, and exercise doses become structured data (`sets/reps/hold/tempo`) instead of strings.
5. **Illustrated moves.** One pose drawing per exercise, consistent and animated in Moves and the player, replacing the category icons.
6. **WCAG 2.2 AA, audited.** Screen-reader player flows, voice control of timers, large text and high contrast.

**Exit criteria:** rep counting reaches ≥ 95 % accuracy against hand-counted sets for each supported move; zero frames leave the device (checked by a network-silence test); a session can be finished without touching the screen; the classic app is deleted.

## v5.0 — THE PLATFORM *(target: Sep 2027)*

**The jump: BackForge stops being a tab on one phone and becomes infrastructure for one person's back health. It works across devices, connects to clinicians, plans in seasons, and is open enough for others to build on.**

1. **Local-first sync across devices, end-to-end encrypted.** CRDT state (Automerge class) syncs through a dumb relay that holds only ciphertext. Start a session on the phone and finish it on the tablet. Data stays readable with no server, works fully offline, and keys never leave your devices.
2. **Health data arrives automatically.** Muse's Health Connect bridge becomes a scheduled, signed push into your encrypted store, with no file to import. Apple Health is added through the same contract.
3. **Clinician mode.** One tap creates an expiring, read-only share link: assessment history, findings, pain trend, adherence, gate status, form-flag clips (your choice) and the reasoning behind every plan choice. The physio annotates, and annotations come back as *suggested* plan changes that you accept. The plan never changes silently. Every plan is reproducible from its inputs and the rules version stamped on it (an audit trail).
4. **Periodised programming.** Gates move you through 4-to-6-week blocks (foundation, capacity, strength, return to activity) with automatic deloads. Templates for desk workers, post-flare rebuilds and athletes returning to sport are built on the findings schema, not generic workout lists.
5. **Opt-in circles.** 2–6 people share streaks and "trained today" check-marks, with paired Live sessions over WebRTC. No feeds and no pain leaderboards. Off by default and removable in one tap.
6. **An open schema and API.** The findings, exercise, voice-line and gate formats are published as a versioned spec, so other apps and researchers can build on them. Export is total and lossless, forever.
7. **Proof.** An opt-in, consent-first anonymised outcomes dataset, and a prospective observational run with a clinic (adherence, pain trajectory, Oswestry/Roland-Morris at 12 weeks). The results are published whatever they show. Clinician-reviewed translations follow (es, hi, ur), with safety copy translated first.

**Exit criteria:** two devices converge after offline edits on both, with no data loss (fuzz-tested); one external physiotherapist reviews a real share link end to end and their findings are filed in the repo; the first interaction takes under 1 s on a mid-range phone, installed and offline; ≥ 99.9 % of sessions are crash-free; every release is gated on the safety suite plus a full assessment-to-plan-to-Live golden-path test.

## Principles (every version, no exceptions)

1. **Safety is enforced by tests, not by disclaimers.** The invariants in `AGENTS.md` §3 are tests. A feature that can't prove it respects them doesn't ship.
2. **The plan is earned by assessment, never by the calendar.** Pain and completion gate progression.
3. **Adherence beats intensity.** Every design review asks one question: does this make it easier to start?
4. **Private by architecture.** Local-first by default. Anything that leaves the device is explicit, encrypted, exportable and deletable. No analytics SDKs, ads or trackers, ever.
5. **Evidence or it's decoration.** Every exercise, test, rule and coach line can be checked. The app screens patterns and trains; it never diagnoses.
6. **Maintainable by more than one mind.** Content is data, rules are written down, and any agent (Muse, Claude, a human) can ship safely by following `AGENTS.md`.
