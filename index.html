Movement assessment

13 short self-tests, no equipment beyond a mat, a chair and a wall. Do them in any order, over as many sittings as you need — answers save automatically, and scoring unlocks after the 6 core tests. This screens movement patterns — it does not diagnose. Retest every 4 weeks; your plan rebuilds itself from the results.

Stop rules — read first

Stop any test if pain spreads further down the leg, or you get new numbness, tingling or weakness. Seek urgent care, do not continue, for saddle/groin numbness, bladder or bowel changes, rapidly worsening leg weakness, or severe unrelenting night pain. Mild effort is fine; sharp, electric or spreading pain is not. Skipping a painful test is useful data — mark it skipped.

Safety & scope

BackForge is a self-guided exercise planner. It screens movement patterns and builds general back-strength programs; it does not diagnose, treat, or replace a physiotherapist. Program guardrails baked into every generated plan: no loaded spinal flexion, no loaded twisting, never both knees to chest at once, no press-ups for extension-intolerant backs, and a modify-don't-stop rule — every exercise ships with a regression. If your symptoms worsen or red flags appear, stop and see a clinician.
